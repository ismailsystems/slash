Slash Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Slash stores your financial list, settings, unfinished add-field draft, and any item reminders you choose locally on your device. Slash has no user account, advertising, analytics, bank connection, or developer-operated server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share the financial entries you put into Slash.

The paid app download and optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase information under its own privacy policy. Slash receives only the StoreKit transaction information needed to process an optional tip.

Optional tips do not unlock features or change your account status. Slash does not operate an account system and does not receive your payment details.

When you choose Remind me for an item, Slash asks iOS to schedule three local notifications. The item name can appear in those notifications, but it is not sent to a developer-operated service. Checking, Slashing, deleting, or choosing Stop reminders cancels the remaining sequence.

When you leave Slash, it covers the screen so your balances are not visible in the system app switcher.

Deleting Slash deletes its locally stored data. If a future version adds sync, analytics, or another form of data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that collection begins.
